Steps

What the tour shows

  1. Welcome"Welcome to shop name. A one-minute tour of your admin." Start or skip.
  2. MenuEverything lives in the menu, grouped by Catalog, Sales, Website and System.
  3. Search ⌘KJump to a product, an order number or a page from the keyboard.
  4. To do todayWhat needs you now: paid orders to ship, payments waiting, items in a cart.
  5. New productEvery piece is unique: one product, one stock number, sold once. Photos and a QR certificate come with it.
  6. OrdersWeb shop and fair sales arrive here; the shipping board shows what to pack.
  7. WebsiteLook, pages and menus of the shop.
  8. PaymentsConnect Mollie to take real payments.
  9. Locked itemsA lock means the module is not in your package. Open it to see what it does.
  10. AccountPassword, two-factor sign-in, and "Restart tour".
  11. Done"You're set." Button: View your shop.

Steps on menu items that are hidden on a phone (closed menu) show as a centred card instead of a spotlight. A step whose target is missing (for example no locked modules on Complete) is left out.

How it works

Rules and build

When it starts

  • Once per owner, on the first admin page after sign-in.
  • Never inside the setup wizard; for a new shop it starts on the first page after the wizard.
  • Skip or finish both count as seen. "Restart tour" in Account (and in the ⌘K palette) plays it again.
  • Superadmins never get it automatically.

Build

  • User.adminTourSeenAt (one column, one migration).
  • A small client component in the admin shell; targets are data-tour="…" attributes on existing elements. No new dependency.
  • Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Esc; focus stays in the card; respects reduced motion.
  • Tests: shows once, not in the wizard, restart works.
Decisions (29 Oct 2026)

Your choices

  1. Every owner gets it.On their first sign-in, including colleagues invited later and owners of migrated shops.
  2. After the setup wizard.It never starts inside the wizard; a new shop sees it on the dashboard after Go live.
  3. Spotlight tour.Skippable, restart from Account or the ⌘K palette.
  4. Stays on the dashboard.Points at the menu and dashboard parts; it does not open other pages.
