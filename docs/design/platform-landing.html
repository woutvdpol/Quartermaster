Questions dealers ask

What happens when the trial ends?

Your storefront closes and visitors see a "temporarily closed" page. Your admin, products and orders stay exactly as they are. Pick a package with us and the shop opens again.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Every shop runs on its own domain. The Quartermaster address is only for signing up and for the network.

Which payment methods can my buyers use?

Payments run through Mollie: iDEAL, Bancontact, cards and PayPal. At the fair you register cash or card payments taken on your own terminal.

Can I move my current shop?

The setup wizard imports your products from a WooCommerce or Shopify CSV export.

Is my shop available in other languages?

Your storefront can run in English, Dutch and German. Translations are drafted on our own servers and only go live after you approve them.
